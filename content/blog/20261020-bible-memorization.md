There is a book found online years ago called <u>How to Memorize the Bible: Training the Memory to Learn Holy Scripture</u> by William Evans. It is a small booklet with time-tested steps to improving memory with scripture memorization in mind. The author himself had memorized the entire Bible in the King James Version as well as the New Testament in the American Standard Version.

<img src="/mycup/bible_memorization.png" alt="How to Memorize the Bible" style="display:block; width:min(88%, 24rem); max-width:100%; height:auto; margin:1.5rem auto; border-radius:8px;">

Over the years, I have started the book and then stopped a number of times - no fault of the book. Once again, I have begun to follow the steps; and this time I hope to stick with it.

So far I have almost committed to memory a list of 100 loosely-related words. Now I get to review them daily for the next month before moving on to the next step :)

Below are some links if you are interested in checking out this little book.

Read it online [here](https://www.preceptaustin.org/how-to-memorize-william-evans), or download a PDF of it [here](https://establishlife.org/wp-content/uploads/dlm_uploads/2019/03/How-to-Memorize-the-Bible.n.pdf). Or order a paper copy on

Until next time.
