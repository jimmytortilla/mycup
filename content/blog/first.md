In 1993 and 1994 I lived in the Santa Cruz on the central coast of California. About once a month a group of young people from local churches and led by a pastor from Santa Cruz Bible Church, went down to the seawall area in Santa Cruz and sang praise songs together, often in the fog that so often drifts in in that part of the coast.

Though I did not really now much about playing the guitar, I was in love with the instrument and wanted to learn. Of course, I could hardly play the simplest song, but my friends knew my passion and found a way to get me involved. I became the string changer.

Usually there where two or three guitars out with the guys pounding -- not strumming but pounding -- away and singing songs like "Lord I Lift Your Name on High" or "Make a Joyful Noise".
