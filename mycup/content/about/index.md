### "My Cup" exists as a site designed to get me focused on written text. It may have occasional pictures or photos, but it is intended as a *space* dedicated to writing down - OK, typing or keying - ideas. I have other sites that serve different purposes, including [family updates](https://stratton.life), [geeky and nerdy stuff](https://jimmytortilla.github.io/natesplace/), [church](https://shiroamu.life), and [social media](https://x.com/strattons2japan).

# Why "My Cup"?

Yes, I know it is a goofy name. For some it may bring to mind the old saying, "That's not my cup of tea." However, that is not where I came up with it. Instead, I thought up the name when I remembered a funny story about the way *my* is used in Japan as a borrowed word. For example, reusable shopping bags are often called "my bag"  (MAI BAGU). "My" here is saying that the object I have is my personal object that I use in a public space, such as at work or at the grocery store.

#### The Story

A number of years ago, not long after my friend had come to Japan, he began working in a public school. As he was brought into the staff room as part of his orientation he was quickly asked by someone *in English*, "Do you have my cup?"

You can guess how that probably went:

"Do I have *what*?"

"Do you have my cup?"

"No, I don't."

"You don't have my cup?"

"No. Why would I have your cup?"

"No, no. Do you have *MAI CAPU*? A cup you can use at school."

And so went another awkward conversation between two strangers in that strange land, or so it seemd to my uninitiated friend.

And so it goes for the title.

I hope you enjoy *My Cup* at least a little bit. But even if it isn't your cup of tea, it is mine.
